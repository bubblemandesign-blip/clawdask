Under the Hood

How ORRERY Works

Everything stays on your computer. Here is exactly what happens when you press Start.

Your Private Automation Engine

ORRERY is not a website. It is a secure, standalone program running directly on your computer hardware. This means your personal files, conversations, and data are physically locked inside your machine.

1. The Control Panel

The interface you interact with is just your remote control. When you select an AI brain (like ChatGPT, Claude, or a free offline Local Model), ORRERY securely encrypts your choice and saves it right on your hard drive.

2. The Local Brain

When you click "Start", ORRERY wakes up a fully private "Brain" running in the background.

  • This Brain cannot be accessed from the outside internet. It only listens to you.
  • It figures out what you want to do (e.g., "Find all the receipts in my Downloads folder and make an Excel file").

3. The Hands (Agents)

Once the Brain understands the goal, it deploys "Agents." Think of Agents as invisible hands that can securely control your computer for you.

  • They can safely read your files, click buttons, or search things online.
  • They use secure, isolated tools so they can only touch what you allow them to touch.

4. The Result

Within seconds, ORRERY finishes the task. Whether you asked it to write an email, fix a piece of software, or summarize a massive PDF, it completes the work autonomously and delivers the results right to your screen.
