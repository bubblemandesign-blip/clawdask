Security and Risks

Transparency on Data Privacy, Agent Autonomy, and responsible usage.

The Risk of Doing Things Automatically

Because ORRERY is connected directly to your computer, it can actually move files, write text, and delete things if you ask it to. This is what makes it so useful, but it also means you need to be careful:

1. Deleting by Mistake

If you tell the assistant to "Clean up my Downloads folder," it might think "clean up" means "delete old files." Because it has real hands, it might actually delete them. Always be very clear and specific when asking it to organize or delete your files.

2. Artificial Intelligence Makes Mistakes

Sometimes, Artificial Intelligence can act very confidently but still be completely wrong. It might misunderstand your question or give you incorrect information. Always double-check important work (like a financial spreadsheet or a legal email) before you send or save it.

Your Privacy is Guaranteed

We built ORRERY because we believe your data should stay yours. Here is our promise:

  • No Tracking: ORRERY never tracks what you click, what you type, or how you use the app.
  • 100% Secure Offline Mode: If you use our free Local AI models (like Llama 3 or GLM-4), ORRERY guarantees that zero information leaves your computer. You can physically unplug your internet router and the assistant will still work perfectly.

How to stay safe

To safely use ORRERY, simply treat it like a very fast, very eager intern. Give it clear instructions, don't let it permanently delete things without asking, and always review its work before considering it finished.
